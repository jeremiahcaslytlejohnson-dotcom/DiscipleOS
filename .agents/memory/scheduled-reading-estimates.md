---
name: Scheduled reading estimates
description: Rules for estimating reading time on individual scheduled reading days.
---

Use the shared standard verse-count estimate for each day's current assigned passages; do not use persisted or reader-specific pace values for day-level labels. Keep estimates off plan-level progress rows.

**Why:** Day estimates should be comparable across Today, plan day lists, Calendar, and structured reading journeys, and should update when passage assignments change.

**How to apply:** Recompute from the day’s assigned book/chapter references and present it as an estimate, not tracked reading time.
