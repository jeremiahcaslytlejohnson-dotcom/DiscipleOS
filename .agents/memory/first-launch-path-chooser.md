---
name: First-launch path chooser
description: Product guidance for the DiscipleOS phone-sized path-selection screen.
---

**Rule:** Explain who each reading path serves and what it does in concise, legible, benefit-oriented copy. On standard phones—including shorter common screens—fit all three full-card choices, the page header, and sign-in access in one viewport with no vertical scrolling. Use responsive sizing to distribute the cards through available height without oversized headers or empty space. Minimize preview-only notices. On exceptionally small devices, never clip inaccessible choices. Preserve the existing entry actions and saved data.

**Why:** The user reported that the compressed rows gave almost no context and left too much empty space.

**How to apply:** Recheck the full first-launch screen, including header, sign-in, and fixed bottom dock, at common and short phone sizes; assert no vertical scroll at standard sizes whenever its layout or copy changes.
